Color Theory Basics
Color theory is the "why" behind colors that look good together.

The 6 basics
The color wheel: colors arranged in a circle. Red, yellow and blue are the main ones. Mixing them makes the rest.
Warm vs. cool: warm colors (red, orange, yellow) feel energetic. Cool colors (blue, green, purple) feel calm.
Color schemes: easy ways to pick colors that match.
Complementary: opposites on the wheel (blue + orange). Bold.
Analogous: neighbors on the wheel (blue + teal + green). Calm.
Monochrome: shades of one color. Simple and clean.
The 60-30-10 rule: 60% main color, 30% second color, 10% accent color. The accent is for things you want to pop, like buttons.
Contrast for reading: text needs to stand out from its background, or people can't read it.
Color mood: colors carry feelings. Green feels natural, red feels urgent, blue feels trustworthy.
Quick check
Before calling a design done:

Using about 3 colors, following 60-30-10?
Is the accent color saved for important stuff?
Is all the text easy to read on its background?
