Elements of Design
The elements are the ingredients of every design. Everything you see on a screen or poster is made from these 7 things.

The 7 elements
Line: a mark from one point to another. Lines split things up or point your eye somewhere.
Shape: a flat area with edges, like a circle or square. Round shapes feel friendly. Sharp shapes feel serious.
Form: a shape that looks 3D. A circle is a shape. Add a shadow and it looks like a ball. That's form.
Color: sets the mood fast. Red feels loud. Blue feels calm.
Value: how light or dark something is. Dark text on a light background is easy to read because the values are very different.
Texture: how a surface feels, or looks like it would feel. Rough, smooth, fuzzy.
Space: the empty area around and between things. Empty space is not wasted. It helps things breathe.
Quick check
Before calling a design done:

Is there enough space so it doesn't feel crowded?
Is the value difference big enough to read the text easily?
Are the shapes consistent (all rounded, or all sharp)?
