Principles of Design
The elements are the ingredients. The principles are the recipe: rules for arranging things so they look good and make sense.

The 6 principles
Contrast: make different things look clearly different. A bright button on a plain page stands out.
Alignment: line things up. Text that all starts on the same edge looks neat. Random placement looks messy.
Repetition: reuse the same styles. If one button is blue and round, make every button blue and round.
Proximity: put related things close together. A photo's caption goes right under the photo, not across the page.
Balance: spread the visual weight so one side doesn't feel heavier than the other.
Hierarchy: show what matters most first. The biggest, boldest thing is what people read first, so make it the most important thing.
Quick check
Before calling a design done:

Can you tell what's most important in 2 seconds? (hierarchy)
Is everything lined up? (alignment)
Do matching things look the same? (repetition)
Are related things grouped together? (proximity)
