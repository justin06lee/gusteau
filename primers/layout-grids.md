Layout & Grids
A grid is a set of invisible lines that help you line things up. It's like the lines on notebook paper, but for a webpage.

The 6 basics
Columns: the page is split into vertical strips. Many websites use 12 columns because 12 splits evenly into halves, thirds and quarters.
Gutters: the small gaps between columns, so things don't touch.
Margins: the empty space around the outside edge of the page.
Snap to the grid: every box, image and block of text lines up with the columns. That's what makes a page look organized.
Reading patterns: people scan pages in a predictable way, often top-left to the right, then down. Put the important stuff where eyes land first.
Mobile layouts: on a phone, columns usually stack on top of each other instead of sitting side by side.
Quick check
Before calling a design done:

Does everything line up to the same columns?
Are the gaps between things consistent?
Does it still look good stacked on a phone?
