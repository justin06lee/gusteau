Typography Basics
Typography is how text looks. Good typography makes words easy and pleasant to read.

The 6 basics
Serif vs. sans serif: serif fonts have little "feet" on the letters (like Times New Roman). Sans serif fonts don't (like Arial). Sans serif usually looks cleaner on screens.
Use 2 fonts max: one for headings, one for body text. More than that looks messy.
Size shows importance: big text = headings. Smaller text = details. Body text on a website is usually around 16px.
Line height: the space between lines. Too tight feels cramped. A bit of extra space makes reading easier.
Line length: lines that stretch too wide are tiring to read. Aim for about 50 to 75 letters per line.
Contrast with the background: dark text on a light background (or the reverse). Light gray on white is hard to read.
Quick check
Before calling a design done:

Two fonts or fewer?
Body text big enough to read comfortably?
Lines not too long or too cramped?
Strong contrast between text and background?
